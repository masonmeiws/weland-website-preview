Welcome Letter

Dear Delegates, Faculty Advisors, and Honored Guests,

Welcome to MUNUC · SFLS! The Model United Nations of the University of Chicago (MUNUC) is proud to partner with WELAND International and Shanghai Foreign Language School (SFLS) to convene our international conference in Shanghai.

Since 1988, MUNUC has been committed to rigorous education in diplomatic negotiation, public address, and institutional collaboration. Our mission is to make complex geopolitical challenges and multilateral policy mechanisms accessible, engaging, and intellectually rewarding for student delegates.

Over this conference weekend, delegates will simulate high-level decision-making bodies, formulating durable policy responses to systemic global dilemmas. Committee debate requires analytical depth, disciplined argumentation, and persuasive negotiation.

A signature hallmark of MUNUC · SFLS is our Continuous Crisis Committees. Simulating smaller, highly dynamic policy bodies with expanded authority, these fast-paced simulations demand immediate crisis resolution balanced with long-term geopolitical statecraft.

On behalf of MUNUC, SFLS, and WELAND International, we thank you for your scholarly commitment. We eagerly anticipate welcoming you to committee in Shanghai.

MUNUC · SFLS Secretariat
