OGMUN · SHSID 2026

Oxford Global Model United Nations at SHSID 2026

4 to 6 April 2026 · Shanghai

Welcome Letter

Dear Delegates, Faculty Advisors, and Colleagues across the MUN Community,

It is our honor to welcome you to OGMUN · SHSID 2026, convening from 4 to 6 April 2026 at Shanghai High School International Division. This conference represents an annual highlight of rigorous scholarly debate and diplomatic inquiry.

Returning to Shanghai for our landmark tenth iteration, OGMUN · SHSID has welcomed more than 3,000 delegates from over 200 leading secondary schools nationwide over the past decade, remaining a premier platform for scholarly debate in East China.

Oxford Global MUN is distinguished by the forward-looking depth of its agendas and its commitment to institutional scholarship. Our dais members from Oxford will be on site in Shanghai to deliver an authentic, challenging diplomatic simulation.

Hosted continuously at Shanghai High School International Division, founded in 1993 within an institution established in 1865, the conference unites Oxford directors with an exceptional student secretariat from SHSID to provide comprehensive academic mentorship throughout the weekend.

Convening approximately 350 delegates across seven English language committees, our conference adheres to three central tenets: a tech-free floor outside unmoderated caucuses to sharpen active listening and spontaneous rhetoric, rigorous evidence-based policy analysis, and principled multilateral collaboration.

We look forward to welcoming you to Shanghai this April for three days of rigorous diplomacy and shared growth.

Secretariat, OGMUN · SHSID 2026January 2026
