Yale Model United Nations China 2026

15 to 17 May 2026 · Shenzhen

Welcome Letter

Dear Esteemed Delegates, Faculty Advisors, and Parents,

Welcome to Yale Model United Nations China 2026! My name is Bryant Pranboonpluk, a sophomore studying Political Science and Psychology at Yale University, and it is my privilege to serve as your Secretary-General. We are delighted to host our 2026 conference from 15 to 17 May in Shenzhen, China.

Working alongside a dedicated team of 12 Yale undergraduates, our priority is to build an intellectual forum that deepens delegates’ analytical command of multilateral diplomacy. Anchored by our theme, “Shaping Global Voices”, YMUN China 2026 provides delegates the space and opportunity to collaborate, critically examine systemic issues, and learn from one another.

Our conference brings together delegates, faculty advisors, and directors worldwide to bridge cultural divides through structured dialogue. Whether delegates are drawn to international diplomacy, scientific developments, or environmental sustainability, our committee lineup provides focused and demanding academic inquiry across all experience levels.

Beyond committee sessions, our program features specialized delegate workshops, Director Office Hours prior to committee work, and the signature “Life at Yale” academic panel, fostering direct intellectual exchange between delegates and our Yale dais.

We are proud to partner once again with WELAND International to convene this conference. We look forward to welcoming you to Shenzhen this May. Visit our portal at ymunchina.org, reach our secretariat at secgen@ymunchina.org, or follow updates on Instagram @ymunchina.

Bryant PranboonplukSecretary-General, YMUN China 2026
