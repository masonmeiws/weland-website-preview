Overview

Founded in 2005 by Peking University students who pioneered Model United Nations in China, WELAND International delivers rigorous academic conferences, multilateral diplomacy workshops, and educational programs for students worldwide.

30+cities supported each year
500+secondary schools in our training
10,000+students on overseas programmes

Our Mission

The advancement of global governance relies on the intellectual rigor and cross-cultural collaborative capacity of emerging generations. As multilateral systems evolve, the depth and reach of global education continue to expand.

We believe authentic global competence arises from independent inquiry into complex public challenges and the constructive capacity to forge consensus across differing worldviews. Grounded in academic excellence, WELAND International provides structured, inclusive diplomatic platforms that prepare future leaders to navigate international frameworks and respect civilizational diversity.

MUN in China

1951
The University of California forms a Model United Nations team, establishing foundational collegiate MUN practices.
1990s
Model United Nations is introduced to Chinese universities and academic communities.
2001
Peking University establishes the first collegiate Model United Nations association in China.
2005
Peking University convenes the inaugural National High School Model United Nations Conference; WELAND International is founded in the same year.
2006
WELAND International organizes the first Chinese secondary school delegation to attend an international Model UN conference in Boston, USA.
2008
The inaugural WE Model United Nations EXPO convenes in Hangzhou, receiving an official congratulatory letter from then UN Secretary-General Ban Ki-moon.

Four-Tier Academic Framework

Regional & Inter-school Networks
Empowering regional secondary schools and societies, supporting established conference networks across Northeast, Central, Southeast, and Northwest China.
Flagship Collegiate China Conferences
Introducing academic curricula and dais teams from premier global universities, fostering advanced cross regional scholarship and diplomatic exchange.
WEMUN EXPO
Asia’s signature comprehensive Model UN platform, bringing together accomplished delegates and academic secretariats worldwide.
International Delegations & Summits
Leading competitive student delegations to premier international conferences across North America, Europe, and the Asia-Pacific.

Institutional Partners

WELAND International maintains longstanding educational collaborations with provincial and municipal education authorities across China, including Shanghai, Hunan, Fujian, Heilongjiang, Shandong, and Inner Mongolia. It collaborates on specialized initiatives with global institutions including the UNDP China Office, UNAIDS, the Organization of African First Ladies for Development, and the China-Africa Business Council.

WELAND International is an active member unit of the China Public Diplomacy Association and the China-Africa Business Council. Its foundational reference work, Shaping Future Leaders: Diplomatic Strategies and Skills in Model United Nations, was published in 2009 and revised in 2022.
